About

Hello. I’m Sankalp.

I’m an engineering student at IIT Bombay, studying Environmental Sciences & Engineering, and I live in Mumbai.

Outside coursework I build software and products, make small interaction experiments, and sing and play guitar.

Different disciplines, same impulse: understand the system, find the interesting edge, build something from it. Most of it starts with curiosity. Some of it eventually becomes useful.

A pattern in the work.

Four projects in four different fields. The thing they share is a habit of saying what a number, a chart or an image is, and what it isn’t.

  1. Product

    Reclaim

    Treats a craving as something to learn from, and reducing as a legitimate path rather than a failed quit. A half-recorded day is never counted as a clean one.

    Reclaim exhibition
  2. Money

    Aureledger

    Shows a balance as the last one observed, with its date and “Not a live balance”. A card’s amount due is not card spending.

    Aureledger exhibition
  3. Markets

    WealthWithSG

    Candles are outlines labelled in text, so colour never carries meaning alone, and every chart is stamped as illustrative.

    WealthWithSG exhibition
  4. Craft

    Sankalp & Shristi Furnishers

    Every render is labelled as a studio render, and products without a verified price never show one.

    Furnishers exhibition
