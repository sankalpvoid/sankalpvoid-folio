About

I study engineering, build software and make music.

Sankalp Kushwaha, Mumbai. The projects in this gallery are the clearest way to see how I work.

Background

I’m an engineering student at IIT Bombay, studying Environmental Sciences & Engineering. Environmental systems are an active research interest: water, wastewater, modelling, treatment and life-cycle assessment.

I also design and build software: products and interfaces, from the logic underneath to the pixels on top.

What shows up in the work

  • Reclaim: product thinking for a behaviour-change problem, where the interesting part is what to do in the hard moment.
  • WealthWithSG: communication design, using a chart as the argument and being strict about what is sample.
  • Sankalp & Shristi Furnishers: a craft business translated into a website, with honest placeholders.
  • Aureledger: a personal-finance ledger that builds itself from bank, card and SIP alerts, with rules that refuse to guess.
  • Experiments: small studies in motion and interaction.

Right now

  • Building Reclaim.
  • Learning markets and equity: business quality, valuation, financial narratives.
  • Experimenting with AI in product interfaces.

Music

Vocals, guitar and production. It’s the other half of how I make things: a song and an interface both live or die on timing.
