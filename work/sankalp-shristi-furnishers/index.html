The challenge

Furniture is made to order. A website has to show what doesn’t exist yet.

The showroom makes custom teak furniture and sells Sleepwell mattresses in Lucknow. So the hero begins where craft begins, with measurement and line, and lets the object arrive.

Design your bed

A drawing that answers every option.

  1. A reference for every design. Each saved design gets a reference such as SSFI-BED-AB12CD and a share link; saved designs never contain contact details.
  2. A labelled drawing, not a fake 3D model. The preview is a layered SVG, labelled “not 3D, not a photograph”.
  3. No price, no stock, no checkout. Starter products are “sample designs”; the route is an enquiry.
Where it stands

A working demonstration.

Storefront, four configurators (bed, sofa, sofa-cum-bed, dining table), enquiry flow and admin, built with Next.js 16, TypeScript and SQLite. The bed’s lines come from the prototype’s own traced geometry, registered to the render.

It is not the showroom’s live site; enquiries and saved designs are kept only temporarily. Products, prices, stock, photography and policies await the owner.
