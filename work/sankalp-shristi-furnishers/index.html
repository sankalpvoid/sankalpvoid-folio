Before · flat illustrationAfter · studio render

Three decisions.

Design your own, with a reference number.

Four configurators (bed, sofa, sofa-cum-bed, dining table) save a design under a reference such as SSFI-BED-AB12CD and a share link that restores it. Saved designs never contain contact details.

A labelled drawing, not a fake 3D model.

An earlier prototype had a rotatable 3D sofa concept. The current preview is a layered SVG that reacts to every option and is labelled “not 3D, not a photograph”. A slot for a real model is reserved.

No price, no stock claim, no checkout.

Starter products are marked “sample design”, so they can’t show a price or “in stock”. The route is an enquiry (exploring, quotation, visit, ready to order), and a private admin lets the owner replace every render with photography.

Status

Status. A working demonstration: storefront, four configurators, enquiry flow and admin, built with Next.js 16, TypeScript and SQLite. Enquiries and saved designs are kept only temporarily. No inventory export existed, so products, prices, stock, photography and policies all await the owner. The bed lines come from the prototype’s own traced geometry, registered to the render.
