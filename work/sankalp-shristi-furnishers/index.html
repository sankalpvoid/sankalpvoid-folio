Sankalp & Shristi Furnishers

A furniture showroom site where a handful of lines slowly become a bed, and where every placeholder admits it is one.

01 — The challenge

Furniture is made to order. A website has to show what doesn’t exist yet.

The showroom makes custom teak furniture and sells Sleepwell mattresses in Lucknow. Pieces are built to a brief, so there is no verified price list, stock count or full photo library to lean on.

The discovery problem: how does someone browse, imagine and specify a piece before visiting, without the site promising more than the workshop has confirmed?

The visual idea: craftsmanship begins as measurement and line. So the hero starts there and lets the object arrive gradually.

03 — Craft, for now in placeholder

The making is still imagined.

The site has an “Art of Making” story: cutting, shaping, building, finishing. Until the workshop is photographed, those stages are studio renders, and the site says so on every one.

04 — Browsing and customising

Decisions that kept it honest.

  1. 01

    Design your own, with a reference number

    Four configurators (bed, sofa, sofa-cum-bed, dining table) save a design under a reference such as SSFI-BED-AB12CD and a share link that restores it. Saved designs never contain contact details.

  2. 02

    A labelled illustration, not a fake 3D model

    An earlier prototype had a rotatable 3D sofa concept. The current preview is a layered SVG drawing that reacts to every option, and it is labelled “not 3D, not a photograph”. A slot for a real model is reserved.

  3. 03

    No price, no stock claim, no checkout

    Starter products are marked “sample design” and unverified, so they cannot show a price or “in stock”. The route is an enquiry (exploring, quotation, visit, ready to order), not a payment.

  4. 04

    The owner can replace everything

    A private admin handles enquiries, products, photos and option lists, so real photography overrides every render without code changes.

05 — Status and reflection

What this is, and what it isn’t yet.

Where it stands

A working demonstration with a storefront, four configurators, an enquiry flow and an admin. It is not the showroom’s live site, and enquiries and saved designs are kept only temporarily.

Constraints

No inventory export existed, so nothing was imported or invented. Products, prices, stock, photographs, policies and opening hours all await the owner.

What I learned

An honest placeholder is a design material of its own. A label, a lighter tone and a clear next step let a prototype feel finished without pretending to be real.
