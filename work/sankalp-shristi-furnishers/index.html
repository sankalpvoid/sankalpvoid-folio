Demonstration · studio renders · not the showroom’s live site

The challenge

Furniture is made to order. A website has to show what doesn’t exist yet.

The showroom makes custom teak furniture and sells Sleepwell mattresses in Lucknow. So the hero begins where craft begins, with measurement and line, and lets the object arrive.

Design your bed

A drawing that answers every option.

Size, headboard, wood finish. Four configurators in all: bed, sofa, sofa-cum-bed and dining table.

Three decisions

Keeping it honest.

01

A reference number for every design.

Each design saves under a reference such as SSFI-BED-AB12CD and a share link that restores it. Saved designs never contain contact details.

02

A labelled drawing, not a fake 3D model.

An earlier prototype had a rotatable 3D sofa concept. The current preview is a layered SVG that reacts to every option and is labelled “not 3D, not a photograph”. A slot for a real model is reserved.

03

No price, no stock claim, no checkout.

Starter products are marked “sample design”, so they can’t show a price or “in stock”. The route is an enquiry, and a private admin lets the owner replace every render with photography.

Status

Where it stands.

A working demonstration: storefront, four configurators, enquiry flow and admin, built with Next.js 16, TypeScript and SQLite. It is not the showroom’s live site, and enquiries and saved designs are kept only temporarily. No inventory export existed, so products, prices, stock, photography and policies all await the owner. The bed lines come from the prototype’s own traced geometry, registered to the render.
