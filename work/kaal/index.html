05 · Desktop companion

Kaal

A little presence.
A considered interruption.

Read the story ↓Meet the crow ↗

macOS app · in development

The question

What if useful began with quiet?

A companion lives beside your work. Every interruption has to earn its place.

01 · At the edge of attention

Present,
without taking over.

The crow is Kaal’s ambient form. Calling Kaal is a separate act; a focused stretch raises the threshold for interruptions.

The character can have a life of its own without demanding yours.

02 · The Observatory

A thought is
not a fact.

The control centre makes the difference visible. What you said, what was kept and what Kaal infers have different names.

A vocabulary of evidence, carried from the brain into the interface.

Said
What you explicitly told Kaal.
Remembered
What has been kept as memory.
Noticed
An observation, with its own label.
Thinks
A hypothesis that can be wrong.
Recommends
A stance, rather than a statement of truth.
03 · Under the surface

The rules
hold the line.

Deterministic logic handles the foundation. An optional local model helps interpret language; it does not become the authority over records or actions.

Electron · React · TypeScript. The portfolio is an explanation of the desktop system, not a browser port.

  1. Read.

    Rules first. A known command has a predictable path.

  2. Interpret.

    An optional Ollama adapter is restricted to a loopback address on the same computer.

  3. Confirm.

    Model proposals pass through normalization and confirmation before changing records.

04 · Design decisions

Attention is borrowed.

Silence is neutral.

Not replying is not evidence of failure. The relationship model counts actual interaction, rather than punishing absence.

Permission is visible.

Awareness and optional connections have controls. Voice begins with a deliberate hold; this exhibition never opens a microphone.

Memory can change.

The Observatory gives corrections, superseded beliefs and forgetting a place in the interface.

The work, today

A companion,
still becoming.

Kaal is a macOS desktop app in private development. This story is based on its current code and original artwork: presence, the Observatory, voice state and the optional local-model boundary.

The crow preview is illustrative. It demonstrates the real artwork, not a live voice, memory or desktop integration. The landscape and emblem are project artwork, not screenshots.
