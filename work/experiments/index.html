The idea

Three small studies. One question each, about motion.

Studies, not products. Previews are screenshots; “Try it here” loads the real page only when you ask, and unloads it when you close it.

A · scroll → canvas

CyberPunk

Can the page behave like a moving sequence instead of a document?

300 frames drawn to a canvas and mapped to scroll, with GSAP ScrollTrigger and Locomotive Scroll. Keeping that sequence smooth beside everything else on the page was the hard part.

B · pointer → depth

Parallax

How little does a flat screen need to feel like a place?

Mountain, fog and light layers move at their own speeds and perspective depths, with a little rotation from the pointer. Simple transforms, no 3D engine. The scene’s photography is stock.

C · timeline → rhythm

GSAP Motion

What makes animations feel orchestrated instead of decorated?

GSAP timelines sequence the opening; ScrollTrigger handles scrubbed sections and cards that enter from a direction. Small changes in overlap and easing change how the whole interface feels. The brand in the page is demo content.
