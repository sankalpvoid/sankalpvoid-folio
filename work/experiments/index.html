Study A · scroll as a sequence

CyberPunk

Can the page behave like a moving sequence instead of a document? 300 frames drawn to a canvas and mapped to scroll, with GSAP ScrollTrigger and Locomotive Scroll.

Study B · pointer as depth

Parallax

How little does a flat screen need to feel like a place? Layers move at their own speeds with a little rotation from the pointer; simple transforms, no 3D engine. Move across the picture to compare the two captured states.
