The idea

A chart is noise until someone shows you what to look for.

WealthWithSG is a trading-education concept whose homepage tells that story as six frames. I designed it and built the prototype in Next.js, with sample content throughout.

Frame 03 · Conflict

Illustrative data · not market data

  1. Up close, noise and information look exactly alike.

  2. Strip the noise away and structure is what remains.

Around the chart

One tool that really works.

A risk-per-trade calculator turns account size, risk and stop distance into a position size. Nothing is stored. Everything else around the chart is structured type and sample content.

Three decisions

What I chose.

01

SVG, not WebGL.

The hero is hand-composed SVG driven by one scroll value, so it stays sharp and light, with separate compositions for desktop (64 small candles) and phones (26).

02

Colour never carries meaning alone.

Candles are hollow outlines, green for up and red for down, each also labelled in text. Every chart is stamped “Illustrative data · not market data”.

03

A story with a way out.

“Explore learning” is available from the first frame and the navigation never leaves. One tool is genuinely interactive: the risk calculator.

Where it stands

A working prototype.

Built in Next.js 15, React 19 and TypeScript. The chart narrative, calculator and glossary work; learning tracks, articles and guides are sample or pending, and social links aren’t connected.

There is no market data: every chart is illustrative, and nothing here is financial advice. The source is private.
