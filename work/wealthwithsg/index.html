The idea

A chart is noise until someone shows you what to look for.

WealthWithSG is a trading-education concept whose live homepage tells that story as six frames. I designed it and built the prototype in Next.js, with sample content throughout.

The live homepage

Six frames, one scroll.

Each frame changes what the same chart is for. Captures of the prototype.

Three decisions

What I chose.

01

SVG, not WebGL.

The hero is hand-composed SVG driven by one scroll value, so it stays sharp and light. It has separate compositions for desktop (64 small candles) and phones (26). The chart on this page rebuilds itself to the viewport the same way.

02

Colour never carries meaning alone.

Candles are hollow outlines, green for up and red for down, and each is also labelled in text. Every chart is stamped “Illustrative data · not market data”, and a risk-disclaimer page exists.

03

A story with a way out.

The journey is long, so “Explore learning” is available from the first frame and the navigation never leaves. One tool is genuinely interactive: a risk-per-trade calculator that turns account size, risk and stop distance into a position size.

Around the chart

Structured type, around the chart.

Status

Where it stands.

A working prototype built in Next.js 15, React 19 and TypeScript. The chart narrative, calculator and glossary work. Learning tracks, articles, the checklist and guides are sample or pending, and social links aren’t connected. There is no market data: every chart is illustrative. Nothing here is financial advice.
